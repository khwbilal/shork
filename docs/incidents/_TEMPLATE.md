# WL-nnn: short title of what broke

- **Work-log item:** [WL-nnn](../work-log.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Severity:** low | medium | high (dev-time cost or blast radius, not a security rating)

## Context

What was being attempted when the problem showed up.

## Symptom

What was actually observed: the error text, the wrong output, the surprising timing. Quote the real message; a future reader searches for it.

## Diagnosis

How the real cause was found, including dead ends worth not repeating.

## Fix

What changed, and why that is the right fix rather than a workaround.

## Prevention / follow-ups

What stops this recurring: a rule in the ruleset, `CLAUDE.md` or memory, a check, a follow-up WL item. Link them.
